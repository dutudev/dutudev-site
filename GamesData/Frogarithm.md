# Frogarithm

Each enemy frog has a number either a quest event on their back. Your main character jumps from one frog to another and takes values accordingly. Completing mathematical challenges rewards players with points or special items. By progressing in the game you get more complex quests and operations even a secret hat.

The game was made at Unihack 2023 in collaboration with David Iancic, Vlad Stoica and Paul Rebegea.

Controls:

W,A,S,D - Frog Movement

Spacebar - Jump
