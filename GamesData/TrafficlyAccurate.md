# Trafficly Accurate

 You find yourself inside a multitude of difficult intersections, although challenging,

 your ability to look into the future helps you predict and evade potential accidents, allowing you to be "**Trafficly Accurate**".

## Controls:

**W A S D - for movement.**

## Tips:

**Try juking every car's action in order to have a clean and easy run.**
