# We Have Lautar At Home

## A Folk Rhythm Game

Step into the shoes of a well known lautar who was met with a terrible fate, his beloved saxophone snaps in half. He has no choice but to play his old forgotten drums. Prove your button pressing skills and bring Romanian folk to modern audiences, his career depends on it.

## Features

-   Osu-like rhythm style game
-   Great art
-   6 Romanian folkloric songs
-   Tactile input system (if you use drums)

## Credits

Game made in collaboration with Sofia Pacurari & Denisa Halmagean at Unihack 7 (2025)
