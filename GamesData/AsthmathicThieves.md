# Asthmathic Thieves

Rob a house as a thief who suffers from asthma, try to steal as much stuff as you can, before getting exposed by cameras or losing your breath  
**Controls :**

A & D - move

W & S - switch between items

E - pickup items

Q - drop items / use inhaler

SPACE - toggle mask

  

Made for GGj 2026 by dutudev & ale :)

  

*note : to register items, you need to drop them in front of the house, do not take items with you when you leave 😭.
