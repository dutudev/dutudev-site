# Fragments Of Freedom

In this game, you are trapped in your own mind. You suffer from schizophrenia, and you must water the plants hidden in this game. But your mind is playing tricks on you! Your memory loss got the best of you, and while you're on the hallway, the rooms next to you might switch places.
