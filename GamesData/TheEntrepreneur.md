# The Entrepreneur

# **Your dream come true :**

You quit your 9 to 5 job to start your own tech company. With 10k dollars to your name and the empty garage of your house, you begin the amazing journey of entrepreneurship.

Work alone, hire people, or try your luck in the stock market and try to take your company from startup to industry leadership. Are you up for the task?

# **Features :**

• Multiple ways to make money

• Hire people to grow your business

• Simulated crypto and stock market

• GAMBLING ...not real though *(this is not legal advice, do not gamble under the legal age)*

• Save system (made by me, might explode)  

• Nice soundtrack to listen to
